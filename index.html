Privacy Policy

TrustScan — QR Code Scanner  ·  Last updated: 28 July 2026

The short version: TrustScan scans codes entirely on your device. We do not run any servers, we have no account system, and we never receive, store, or sell your scans. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the single banner advert — and you can remove it by upgrading to Pro.

1. Who we are

TrustScan ("the app") is developed and published by TrustScan ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at [YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL].

2. What the app does with your data

DataWhyWhere it goes
Camera image To detect QR codes and barcodes in the live preview Never leaves your device. Frames are analysed in memory and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, photographed, or uploaded.
Scan history So you can find codes you scanned earlier Stored only on your device in a local database. You can delete any entry, clear everything, or switch history off entirely in Settings.
Photos you choose To scan a code from an existing image, and to add a logo to codes you create Read on your device only. We never upload your photos.
Codes you create To save a generated QR/barcode image to your gallery Written to your own device storage, at your request.
Link safety checks To warn you about suspicious or malicious links Performed 100% offline. The checks run against rules built into the app. Scanned links are not sent to us or to any service.

3. We do not collect personal data

  • No account, sign-up, email address, or phone number is required.
  • We operate no servers and receive no data from the app.
  • We use no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information — we do not have any to share.

4. Advertising (the one exception)

The free version of TrustScan shows a single banner advert supplied by Google AdMob. To serve it, the app must connect to Google, and Google may process device information such as your advertising identifier, approximate location derived from IP address, and device type. This is the only situation in which the app uses the internet.

Your scans, scan history, and camera data are never shared with advertisers. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can stop this entirely by:

  • Upgrading to Pro, which removes the advert, or
  • Resetting or opting out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Opening links

When you choose to open a scanned link, it is handed to your browser or another app on your device. Once you leave TrustScan, that destination's own privacy policy applies. TrustScan warns you first if a link shows signs of being unsafe, but you remain in control of whether to open it.

7. Children

TrustScan is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your rights and control

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: delete individual scans, clear all history, turn history off for incognito scanning, or uninstall the app — which removes all locally stored data permanently. We hold no copy to delete.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the revised version at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
